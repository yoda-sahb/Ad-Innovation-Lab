WHY THIS EXISTS

Better intelligence changes the possible.

Models and agents can reason across more context, outcomes, time, and action spaces than traditional advertising systems were designed around.

System boundaries still decide what works.

Transaction rails, incentives, ownership, authority, latency, privacy, and evidence determine whether a clever idea becomes a useful product.

Where does the system actually need to change, and where can the existing system absorb the value?

PUBLIC / PRIVATE BOUNDARY

Private is the default.

The working lab includes mechanisms, architectures, prototypes, prior-art analysis, experiments, company overlays, and unpublished ideas. Public artifacts are deliberately sanitized derivatives. A detail is not published merely because it is interesting or technically portable.

A public page should make the quality of the reasoning legible without making the invention reproducible.
