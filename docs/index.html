THE THESIS

Intelligence is getting wider.

Models can reason across intent, commerce, creative, context, measurement, prior exposure, customer state, channel, and time.

The rails are still narrow.

Much of advertising still asks that intelligence to express itself through isolated transactions, fragmented ownership, and local optimization.

Where is intelligence being constrained by the system around it, and what is the smallest system change worth making?

THE METHOD

Research should end in a decision.

PossibilityFind the structural problem.
MechanismDefine the smallest causal change.
ProofCompare against a real baseline.
Kill testWrite down what makes us stop.
PrototypeResolve a real uncertainty.
EvidenceSeparate causal value from plausibility.
Commit / KillBuild, narrow, or stop.

A prototype is not proof. A compelling diagram is not proof. A new standard is not proof.

WHAT WE DO NOT BUILD

Novelty is not a business case.

AI wallpaperAdding a model to an unchanged workflow.
Protocol firstAsking an ecosystem to coordinate before one bounded decision proves value.
Demo theaterTreating a prototype as evidence of causal economics.
Architecture vanityDesigning a new exchange because the diagram is elegant.
Elephant assumptionsDepending on another actor to surrender economics or incentives for global efficiency.
Zombie thesesKeeping the idea alive after the kill condition fails.

THE CEO ROUTE

Bring the problem that keeps coming back.

  1. 01What is the root problem, not the feature request?
  2. 02What business invariants cannot be compromised?
  3. 03At what system layer should the problem actually be solved?
  4. 04What is the smallest portable mechanism worth testing?
  5. 05What experiment proves incremental value?
  6. 06What result kills the thesis?
