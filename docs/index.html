WHY THIS EXISTS

AI makes more things possible.

Models and agents can reason across intent, commerce, creative, context, outcomes, time, and increasingly complex action spaces.

Old system assumptions still matter.

Advertising remains shaped by transaction rails, ownership boundaries, incentives, local optimizers, delayed evidence, and fragmented authority.

Where does better intelligence actually need a new system, and where can the old system absorb it just fine?
