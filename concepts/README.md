# Public Research Themes

> **This is the sanitized public layer of a private personal R&D lab.**

[← Ad Innovation Lab](../README.md) · [Lab Method](../LAB_METHOD.md) · [Visual View](../docs/index.html)

The working lab contains substantially more detail than this repository. Public pages are intentionally limited to problem classes, high-level theses, and the questions that make the research interesting.

## Decisioning across time

Advertising systems make many locally intelligent decisions. A broader research question is whether decision quality changes when the system can reason about what earlier advertising has already accomplished rather than treating each opportunity in isolation.

**Public question:** When does memory create incremental economic value rather than simply adding complexity?

## Distributed intelligence

The open advertising ecosystem contains useful intelligence across publishers, buyers, commerce systems, measurement providers, and platforms. Those actors do not necessarily want to centralize their raw data, models, or authority.

**Public question:** Can independently owned intelligence contribute to a decision while ownership and decision rights remain distributed?

## Agent authority and accountability

AI agents can increasingly plan and execute advertising work. Execution capability is only part of the system problem.

**Public question:** What must be true about authority, constraints, accountability, and evidence before autonomous systems can make financially consequential decisions safely?

## Market and transaction rails

Better models do not automatically require new market structure. Existing advertising rails may absorb a surprising amount of new intelligence.

**Public question:** How do you prove that the transaction layer itself is the constraint before inventing a new one?

## What is intentionally absent

This public layer does not publish:

- detailed mechanisms or architecture;
- schemas, algorithms, state models, or decision logic;
- prototype code;
- detailed experiments or kill thresholds;
- prior-art or patent analysis;
- unpublished concept inventory;
- company-specific overlays; or
- restricted/confidential material.

Those remain in the private working lab unless a specific derivative is explicitly cleared for publication.

## Standard

A public page should make the problem and quality of reasoning legible without making the underlying invention reproducible.
